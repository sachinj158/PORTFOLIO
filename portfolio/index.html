My work on ClearTrade focuses on applying agentic AI to trade finance workflows, where getting the right context and surfacing exceptions matter to the people reviewing a case.

Engineering focus
Use retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to ground responses in relevant material and tool calling to connect AI workflows to backend capabilities.
Product objective

Portfolio concept · In progress

Trade Rule Engine

A concept for evaluating structured trade data against versioned rules, with an explanation for each result and a review path for exceptions.

Proposed design
Use AI to help extract and organize inputs. Evaluate explicit rules in a deterministic service, and route ambiguous cases for human review.
What to validate
Use synthetic cases to test rule changes, missing fields and the clarity of exception explanations.
